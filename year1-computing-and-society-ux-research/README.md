# UX Research Case Study: Meta Social Commerce

**Module:** CMPC105 Computing and Society, Year 1 (2024–25), University of Roehampton  
**Type:** Individual research presentation (slides + in-class presentation)

## What I did
- **Platform analysed:** Meta's social-commerce features: Instagram Shops, Facebook Marketplace, product tagging, Messenger/DM commerce and AI-driven ads
- **Explore method:** built a user **persona** (international business student in London) covering goals, motivations, frustrations and expectations, based on user interviews
- **Journey map:** five stages from *Discover → Explore → Engage → Decide → Purchase*, highlighting trust and seller-response pain points
- **Usability test:** think-aloud test with two tasks: (1) find a product via the Shop tab; (2) message a seller about price and delivery
- **Findings:** navigation breaks down after moving into DMs; prices are often missing from posts
- **Intellectual property:** proprietary platform software and APIs, seller content licensing, trademarks, takedown enforcement
- **Legal, ethical and social issues:** privacy and profiling for targeted ads, fraud and fake sellers, algorithmic bias favouring larger brands, seller labour
- **Environmental impact:** local buying (positive) vs. server and AI energy use (negative); no carbon information shown to users
- **Recommendations:**
  - *Easy wins:* pin price/FAQ to posts; auto-reply when sellers are unavailable
  - *Sound investments:* buyer protection for cash-on-delivery; product carbon-impact labels

## Skills demonstrated
User research (personas, journey mapping), think-aloud usability testing, evidence-based UX recommendations,
IP / legal / ethical analysis of a digital platform, IEEE referencing.
